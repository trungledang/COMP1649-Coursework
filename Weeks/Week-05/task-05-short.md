# Task 5 Prototype Idea and Research Questions

COMP1649 Human Computer Interaction

## Prototype idea

I plan to design an AI-assisted study planner for university students with ADHD, under Topic A. Students can upload an assignment brief, check the requirements identified by the assistant and answer questions about missing information. The assistant then suggests smaller steps and a study schedule, which students can edit before saving.

Students can mark steps as complete and ask for help when they are unsure what to do next. The interface will focus on clear next steps, simple navigation and easy access to support. The Axure prototype will use simulated AI responses for example assignments.

## Research questions

1. How easily can university students with ADHD identify what to do first from the prototype's suggested task breakdown?

2. How easily can university students with ADHD change a suggested study schedule to fit their available time without help?

3. How easily can university students with ADHD find and use the help feature to decide what to do next when a task feels difficult?

These questions focus on the usability of the prototype. A proposed usability study would observe task completion, time taken and requests for help, alongside students' feedback on clarity and ease of use.
