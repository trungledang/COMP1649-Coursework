Use of Generative AI in this Assessment
Generative AI may be used only to support preparation for this assessment.
Permitted uses include learning support, planning, brainstorming, clarification,
comprehension, feedback, and language improvement. AI must not generate
substantive assessment content.
All analysis, arguments, interpretations, and the final submission must be your
own work.
At the end of your assignment, before the references, you should write a
reflection on your use of AI: 60-100 words, covering:
 AI tool(s) used (e.g., Microsoft Copilot).
 How AI supported your learning
 How AI outputs were edited, transformed, or expanded.
 How outputs were verified and inaccuracies corrected.
Submitting AI-generated work as your own will be treated as assessment misconduct.

Assessment Specification
Choose one of the following topic areas for your coursework:
A. Design a digital application (e.g. a mobile app) for neurodiverse learners in a
higher education setting. Potential features may include: task breakdown
and time management tools, interface customisation, mood tracking, peer
support etc. Accessibility elements could be considered.
B. Design a digital application (e.g. a mobile app combined with a smart watch
app) for divers, swimmers, fishers, and sailors to report debris at sea and
other environmental problems they encounter on the water. Potential
features may include: logging debris sightings, alerting relevant authorities,
and sharing information with fellow water sport enthusiasts and volunteer
groups. Wearables connected to an app and outdoor interaction design could
be considered.
C. Design a digital application (e.g. a tablet app or mobile app) for children to
encourage reading children’s stories and books (could be digital books or
printed books). Potential features may include: search and reading
recommendations, guided (interactive) reading, summarising and quiz
activities, progress indicators and rewards, etc. Gamification elements could
be considered.
You have been commissioned to write a report and create a prototype for a new
interactive product for one of the above options and target groups. Investigate the

needs and context of the chosen target group and the product, and decide on two to
three main features, which are supported by your literature research.
The scope of the interactive prototype should focus on two to three main areas. You
are asked to create a proof of concept for the interactions in this system to see if
users find it usable and desirable. The basic brief is open for interpretation, and you
can and should design desirable interactions as you see fit based on your
background readings and your research activities.
Interactive prototype
You need to create a mid-fidelity prototype of the application that enables people to
experience at least the core user journeys/scenarios (two or three) that are available
in your application in an interactive manner. This prototype should be developed in
Axure RP or Figma Design (not Figma Make), unless agreed otherwise with the
module leader.
You are required to describe the conceptual design and provide a description and
visualisation of the product and its components and interactions in the report. You
need to submit an interactive digital prototype demonstrating the interface and
interactions of your application and explain how a user interacts with its components.
Your design and research activities need to be in alignment with your target group
and justifications for all your assumptions and design decisions need to be provided.
Report
At the beginning of your report, you need to state your choice of topic from the above
options. In the coursework report, you document your research and design activities,
and the required future research study and reflections on your learning. This includes
an annotated bibliography that will inform your design, a discussion of the design for
the product, and a discussion of how design principles will be applied. The report will
also discuss your (design) process of developing the interactive mid-fidelity prototype
and how relevant HCI theory has been implemented. A plan for an empirical
research study should be proposed to investigate a research question or hypothesis,
and the conclusion should reflect on how HCI might influence your future practice.
More details can be found in the assessment criteria below. You may also want to
consult the annotated table of contents available on the COMP1649 Moodle page to
help you structure your report.
Your report needs to be professionally and academically written and structured,
based on your own research and reading, and written by yourself using appropriate
in-text citations and referencing. This includes the demonstration of English
language proficiency, appropriate level of detail, professional formatting of the report,
and the writing should be supported by at least 8 relevant academic references
(journal papers, conference papers, academic books - not blogs or online tutorials
etc.). References and in-text citations should be formatted in Harvard style.

The report word limit is 3000 words. Submissions that exceed this limit by more than
10% will incur a mark deduction. Any content beyond this threshold will not be
considered for assessment.
